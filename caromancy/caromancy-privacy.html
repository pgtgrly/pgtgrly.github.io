The game does not ask for your name, postal address, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise GPS location. It does not require a CAROMANCY account.

Your choices

  • Gameplay analytics and crash reports: off by default. Open Settings and change “Share gameplay statistics” at any time. Turning it off stops new gameplay analytics events and app-generated error submissions. Crash reports can remain on your device until a later launch with sharing already on. If a launch starts with sharing off and you turn it on later, older pending crash reports are discarded. Turning sharing off cannot recall reports already sent or a transfer already requested.
  • Advertising privacy: the game refreshes consent information before it requests an ad. Where a privacy message or options form is required, use Settings > Ad Privacy to review or change the choices available for your region.
  • Rewarded ads: watching an ad to continue a run is optional. You can end the run and start another without watching one.
  • Game Center: use the Leaderboards button to sign in. You can manage Game Center identity and visibility in iOS Settings.
  • Local game data: using iOS’s Delete App action removes the app and its local settings, history, seeds, progression, and checkpoint. The separate Offload App action keeps documents and data for a later reinstall. Game Center records and data already processed by third-party services are managed separately.

CAROMANCY currently has no player account or cloud-save service, so there is no separate in-game account to delete. If you contact us about data, include enough detail for us to understand the request. Pseudonymous SDK data may not be possible for us to connect back to you.

Retention and security

Local game data remains on your device until the app clears it or you delete the app. Support messages are kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond, keep a record of the request, protect the service, or meet legal obligations.

Google and Apple set retention periods for information processed by their services. For example, Firebase states that Crashlytics keeps crash traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal from live and backup systems. Their current policies describe other retention periods and controls.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Children

The game is not designed to collect a child’s name, contact details, or precise location. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child sent personal information through a support request, email us so we can review and delete it where applicable.

International processing and disclosures

Google, Apple, and email providers may process information in countries other than your own. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect users or the service, investigate abuse or fraud, or complete a business transfer subject to appropriate protections.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to ask for access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or a copy of personal information we control, or to object to certain processing. You may withdraw consent for future optional gameplay analytics and crash reporting through the in-game setting, and change applicable advertising choices through Ad Privacy.

Email pranavgarg@gmail.com to make a request. We may need to verify the request and may be unable to locate pseudonymous third-party SDK records from the information you provide. You can also contact the privacy or data-protection authority available in your region if you have a concern about how a request is handled.
