Quick start

How do I control the paddle?
Swipe left or right anywhere on the screen to orbit the paddle. Hold near either edge to keep turning. Lift and touch again anywhere; the paddle does not jump to your finger.
How do I use Blast?
Build charge through play. A ready ball glows mint. Double-tap the playfield or tap the Blast button to detonate around it; blast-radius upgrades and temporary powers change the area.
What do jump pads and portals do?
Jump pads launch balls over low walls toward airborne targets. Violet portals move a ball to the linked exit and point it along the exit arrow.
What happens after wave 10?
Wave 10 records a victory. You can finish the run there or continue into an endless sequence with stronger combinations.

Runs and saves

How do I enter or replay a seed?
From the main screen tap Choose Run, select Free Orbit or Remix Orbit, and enter letters, numbers, or dashes in Replay Seed. Daily Orbit uses its fixed daily seed. Recent runs in the Journal also have a Replay Seed action.
Why can’t I edit the Daily seed?
Everyone receives the same UTC-dated Daily Orbit setup, so its seed and core choices are fixed.
Can I resume after closing the app?
The game saves a recovery checkpoint around run boundaries and when the app moves to the background. If one is available, the main screen shows Continue Run. An interrupted active wave may restart from its saved wave boundary and is marked as restored.
What stays after a run?
Upgrades reset. Records, discoveries, earned paddle colours, recent runs, mastery progress, and settings stay on this device.

Scores and Game Center

Which scores reach the public leaderboard?
Only clean ten-wave clears using Standard difficulty and Open Orbit rules qualify. Practice, Relaxed, Mastery Course, rewarded-continue, and restored runs still appear locally but do not submit to the competitive leaderboard.
Why did my score stay local?
Check the run’s result tags and your Game Center sign-in. You can sign in or open the board from Settings > Leaderboards. Network or Game Center availability can also delay or prevent submission.
Why choose a score upgrade?
Score upgrades improve the value of successful target progress and compound with your run multiplier. They are strongest when you can keep balls active and maintain a long scoring line; they do not make survival easier by themselves.

Ads, privacy, and troubleshooting

Do I have to watch an ad?
No. A rewarded ad is an optional way to continue once after a lost run. You can end the result and start a new run for free.
The rewarded continue did not work.
A reward is granted only after the ad provider confirms completion. Keep the app in the foreground until the ad closes. If no ad is available or it fails, your revive remains available; retry later or start a fresh run.
How do I report a bad ad?
Use the ad’s information or AdChoices control when available. You can also email support with the date and time, your country, the advertiser or product shown, and what the ad did. A screenshot helps, but avoid including private notifications or personal information.
How do I change privacy choices?
Open Settings. “Share gameplay statistics” controls optional Firebase gameplay analytics and crash reporting. Ad Privacy opens the choices provided for your region when that entry point is required.
The game looks pixelated or runs slowly.
Open Settings > Visual Quality. Automatic adapts to device load; High prioritizes detail and Low prioritizes frame rate. Close other demanding apps, let the phone cool, and turn off Low Power Mode if performance remains limited.
Sound, motion, or haptics are uncomfortable.
Settings includes master, music, and effects volume; Reduced Motion; turning speed; direction; and haptic feedback. Adjust them before continuing to play.

Privacy requests

The game has no separate player account. Local progress can be removed with iOS’s Delete App action; Offload App keeps documents and data. For questions about analytics, advertising, Game Center, or a support message you sent, read the privacy choices section or email support.
