# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Players.** They enjoyed one of the studio's titles and want the others, or want to follow the studio.
- **Press, platforms and partners.** They need the studio's facts, its titles, press kits and a contact.

The owner wants the studio page to serve both groups equally (20 Nov 2026).

## Product Purpose

pranavgarg.in is the personal site of Pranav Garg and the home of **damagedCronJob Studios**, his one-person indie studio. Each title has its own official page here with facts, guides and support. The studio page gathers the studio's games and apps under one name, so a player can find the next one and a journalist or partner can find the facts.

## Positioning

The owner's words for what the studio makes: "fun content I like".

The page makes no further public promises; the owner declined fair-play, no-account, one-person and craft claims as studio-wide pledges (20 Nov 2026). Individual title pages may still state their own facts.

## Operating Context

- **Hosting.** A static site on GitHub Pages (Jekyll, repository pgtgrly/pgtgrly.github.io) with the custom domain pranavgarg.in.
- **Page structure.** Each title has a self-contained folder of plain HTML with its own style.css: `magnetmob/`, `drizzl/`, `afterarc/`, `lexora/`. `apps/` lists them all.
- **Analytics.** Google Analytics loads through `_includes/analytics.html`.
- **App Store links.** They carry campaign tokens (`pt=128357174&ct=<page>-2026&mt=8`).
