Effective 12 September 2026

AFTERARC does not ask for your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise GPS location. It does not require an account. Replay seeds and purchase receipts are not included in gameplay telemetry.

Your choices

  • Gameplay statistics and crash reports: sharing is off by default. Change “Share gameplay statistics” in Settings at any time. Turning it off stops new app-defined events and error submissions; information already sent cannot be recalled.
  • Advertising privacy: use Settings > Ad Privacy to review or change the choices provided for your region. The game checks the applicable consent status before requesting an ad.
  • Rewarded continues: watching an ad to continue a lost run is optional. You can end the run and start another without watching one.
  • Remove Ads: the optional one-time purchase disables game advertising and includes the same once-per-run continue without an ad. Restore Purchases checks the Apple Account entitlement. The price shown by the App Store is localized for your storefront.
  • Game Center: signing in and using the leaderboard is your choice. Apple controls Game Center identity, visibility, and records.
  • Local game data: deleting the app removes its local settings, history, seeds, progression, and checkpoint. Offloading the app keeps its documents and data for a later reinstall.

AFTERARC has no separate player account or cloud-save service. Pseudonymous analytics or advertising records held by third parties may not be possible for us to connect back to you.

Retention and security

Local game data remains on your device until the app clears it or you delete the app. Support messages are kept only as long as reasonably needed to answer the request, protect the service, or meet legal obligations. Google and Apple set retention periods for information processed by their services.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Children

The game is not designed to collect a child’s name, contact details, or precise location. If a child sends personal information through support, a parent or guardian can email us so we can review and delete it where applicable.

International processing

Google, Apple, GitHub, and email providers may process information in countries other than your own. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect users or the service, investigate abuse or fraud, or complete a business transfer subject to appropriate protections.

Your rights and contact

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to ask for access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or a copy of personal information we control, or to object to certain processing. You can withdraw optional gameplay analytics and crash-reporting consent in Settings and change available advertising choices in Ad Privacy.

Email pranavgarg@gmail.com with a privacy request. We may need to verify it, and third-party pseudonymous records may not be identifiable from the information provided. You can contact your local data-protection authority if you have a concern.
