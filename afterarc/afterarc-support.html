Quick start

How do I control the paddle?
Swipe left or right anywhere on the screen to orbit the paddle. Hold near either edge to keep turning. Lift and touch again anywhere; the paddle does not jump to your finger.
How do I launch the ball?
Tap the arena when the launch prompt is shown. After launch, follow the return angle and move the paddle into the ball’s path.
How do I use Blast?
Build charge through play. A ready ball glows mint. Double-tap the playfield or tap Blast to detonate around it; Blast-radius upgrades and temporary powers change the area.
What do jump pads and portals do?
Jump pads launch balls over low walls toward airborne targets. Linked portals move a ball to the exit and point it along the exit arrow.
What happens after wave 10?
Wave 10 records a victory. You can finish the run there or continue into an endless sequence with stronger combinations.

Runs and saves

How do I enter or replay a seed?
From the main screen tap Choose Run, select Free Orbit or Remix Orbit, and enter letters, numbers, or dashes in Replay Seed. Recent runs in the Journal also have a Replay Seed action. Daily Orbit uses its fixed daily seed.
Can I resume after closing the app?
The game saves a recovery checkpoint around run boundaries and when the app moves to the background. If one is available, the main screen shows Continue Run. An interrupted active wave may restart from its saved wave boundary and is marked as restored.
What stays after a run?
Wave upgrades reset. Records, discoveries, earned paddle colours, recent runs, mastery progress, and settings stay on this device.
How do I leave a run?
Open Pause and choose Exit Run. The result screen also includes an Exit option so you can return to the main screen without starting a new run.

Scores and Game Center

Which scores reach the public leaderboard?
Only clean ten-wave clears using Standard difficulty and Open Orbit rules qualify. Practice, Relaxed, Mastery Course, rewarded-continue, and restored runs still appear locally but do not submit to the competitive leaderboard.
Why did my score stay local?
Check the run’s result tags and your Game Center sign-in. You can sign in or open the board from Settings > Leaderboards. Network or Game Center availability can also delay or prevent submission.
Why choose a score upgrade?
Score upgrades make a strong run more valuable and can support progression goals, but they do not make survival easier by themselves. Choose one when your current build can protect a long scoring line; otherwise take the upgrade that helps you stay alive.

Ads, purchases, and privacy

Do I have to watch an ad?
No. Watching a rewarded ad to continue after a loss is optional. Non-owners may see a banner only on a meaningful results screen. After the first two qualifying results are free of full-screen ads, an interstitial may appear when leaving result 3, then every third qualifying result, subject to availability, consent, and cooldown. A qualifying run is a non-practice run of at least 30 seconds; the cadence also waits for 180 seconds of cumulative eligible play and five minutes after any full-screen ad. A result uses at most one passive ad format, and no banner covers the board.
How do I remove ads or restore a purchase?
Open Settings and choose Remove Ads. The App Store shows the current localized price before confirmation. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It removes results banners and between-run interstitials and makes the same once-per-run continue available without an ad. Use Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account after reinstalling or changing devices.
The rewarded continue did not work.
A reward is granted only after the ad provider confirms completion. Keep the app in the foreground until the ad closes. If no ad is available or it fails, your continue remains available; retry later or start a fresh run.
How do I change privacy choices?
Open Settings. Share gameplay statistics controls optional Firebase gameplay analytics and crash reporting. Ad Privacy opens the choices provided for your region when that entry point is required.
The game looks pixelated or runs slowly.
Open Settings > Visual Quality. Automatic adapts to device load; High prioritizes detail and Low prioritizes frame rate. Close other demanding apps, let the phone cool, and turn off Low Power Mode if performance remains limited.
Sound, motion, or haptics are uncomfortable.
Settings includes master, music, effects, Reduced Motion, turning speed, direction, and haptic feedback. Adjust them before continuing to play.
