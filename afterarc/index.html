What is AFTERARC: Ricochet?
AFTERARC is an orbital arcade game for iPhone and iPad. Move a crescent paddle around the arena, return the ball into crystal targets, choose upgrades, and adapt to bosses and hazards.
What makes AFTERARC a skill challenge?
Your catches shape the next shot. Aim a center or edge return, read moving targets, and adjust to each boss. Standard and Relaxed modes let you choose the pace.
Can I try different upgrade builds?
Yes. Draft upgrades after waves to add balls, widen your paddle, slow the action or strengthen Blast. Replay a run code with different choices and see how the build changes your approach.
How do I control the paddle?
Swipe to steer and tap to launch. A catch near the center sends a straighter return; a catch near the edge bends the shot. Double-tap the arena when Blast is charged.
What changes from run to run?
Draft upgrades that add balls, widen your paddle, slow the action, or strengthen Blast. Portals, lift pads, moving formations, and bosses change the route. Standard and Relaxed modes let you choose the pace.
Can I replay a run?
Replay a run code with another build, try the daily run, or unlock challenge rules after your first wave-10 clear. The Journal records discoveries and opens focused practice.
Does AFTERARC work offline?
The core game works offline. An optional rewarded recovery requires an ad unless you have the one-time Remove Ads purchase. No account is required.
Is AFTERARC free?
It is free to download, with ads and an optional one-time Remove Ads purchase. Once per run, the optional recovery offers Continue or, when available, Rewind. The App Store has current purchase details.
