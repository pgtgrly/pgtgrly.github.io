## Direction contract

THESIS: Every title hangs on the studio's pegboard wall like a toy in its blister pack, and a visitor picks one off the wall. The page refuses the category default, a dark hero over a grid of equal key-art cards.

OWN-WORLD:
- **Ground:** warm perforated hardboard (`#B98A5E` range) with real holes on a 32 px pitch. That pitch is the page's only grid; every pack, tag and gap snaps to it.
- **Packs:** die-cut printed backing cards with a hang tab and euro-slot hole, each in its title's own colours (Magnet Mob royal blue and gold; Drizzl night purple and teal; AFTERARC deep teal and mint; Lexora cream and green). Each carries a clear glossy blister holding real game art.
- **Labels:** starburst stickers (FREE, OUT NOW) printed on the card. Paper price tags for navigation. A white shelf-edge price strip closes the viewport.
- **Type:** heavy rounded display lettering, set angled per card, one shout per card. A plain humanist sans for facts.
- **Colour:** status is a printed sticker on the card, never a UI badge.

STORY:
- **Visitor understands:** this is one person's studio and these are its games, out on the App Store.
- **Believes:** they are made with care, because the page itself is a crafted object.
- **Does:** taps a pack to flip it, then taps "Download on the App Store", or goes to the shelf strip for press facts and contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT (1440×900):
- **Studio header card:** top-left, hung on a peg (logo slot, "damagedCronJob STUDIOS", and a bone note card "Indie games by Pranav Garg").
- **Magnet Mob pack:** largest, centre-left (about 380×660 at 1440), with its App Store button printed on the card. This is the primary action.
- **Other packs:** Drizzl and AFTERARC hang top-right; Lexora hangs lower-right.
- **Navigation:** paper tags "Games / Press / Contact" on pegs at top-right.
- **Shelf strip:** a white shelf-edge strip at the bottom with the press-kit and contact link.

Phones: the wall becomes a vertical aisle, with packs two-up, Magnet Mob full width first, and the header card above.
