Magnet Mob: Crowd Army Runner

Magnet Mob is a crowd runner game for iPhone where you grow an army at number gates and use a magnet to pull enemies in and push them back.

About the game

Magnet Mob starts where crowd runners start. You steer your army, pick the bigger number gate and watch it grow. Then the game gives you a magnet. Attract pulls a pack of enemies together and crushes them. Repel pushes them back and sends their arrows back at the archers who shot them.

The Iron Warlord steals the Lodestone from the village of Millbrook and runs off through rifts in time. You follow through 30 story levels in three eras: the Iron Realm, the Chrome Rift and the Bronze Age. On the way you pull down archer towers and face six bosses. Every boss shows its attack before it lands. Shoot a lock gate open and march through, and your army changes. It can become Iron Guard, Overdrive, Mech Titan or Titan Fusion, where the whole army turns into one giant.

Between runs you rebuild the 12 buildings of Millbrook with the gold you win. Four keepers in the village make wishes that pay gold. After the story comes Conquest. Rift Run is the endless mode. It opens after level 10 and has a new road every week. After each big fight you pick one of three cards.

Features

  • Two magnet powers, Attract and Repel, that answer every threat.
  • Four transformations, including the Mech Titan and Titan Fusion.
  • Six bosses across three eras. Each one shows its attack first and has a weak moment.
  • A village to rebuild, with heroes: Wren, Sir Bram, Vex and Ione.
  • Rift Run: a new road every week, about four to six minutes a run.
  • Fair by design: every danger warns first, nothing paid is random, and paying never makes a level harder.
  • One finger, portrait, about a minute a level.

Why a magnet

Most crowd runners give you one job: steer to the better gate. Magnet Mob gives you a magnet as well. Attract pulls a pack of enemies together and crushes it, and Repel pushes danger back, arrows included, so a fight is about timing as well as numbers.

Heroes and places

  • Wren is the gatekeeper who carries a chip of the Lodestone and gives you the magnet.
  • Sir Bram is Millbrook's loud, loyal knight.
  • Vex is quick and sly, from the metal city.
  • Ione is calm and grand, from the bronze shore.
  • The Iron Warlord steals the Lodestone from Millbrook.
  • Millbrook is the village you rebuild with the gold you win.
  • The King's Road is the map with chests along the way, from level 12.
  • The three eras are the Iron Realm, the Chrome Rift and the Bronze Age.
