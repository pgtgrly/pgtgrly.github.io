Magnet Mob: Crowd Army Runner

Magnet Mob: Crowd Army Runner is a game for iPhone, iPad and Android made by Pranav Garg. On Google Play it is published under the developer name DamagedCronJob. This policy explains what stays on your device, what may leave it, and the choices you have.

Last updated 12 November 2026

The essentials · What the game handles · Your choices · Service providers · Retention · Children · Your rights

The essentials

  • Magnet Mob has no account or login. We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • Your progress, settings and the purchases you own are saved on your device. On iPhone and iPad, the game also keeps a copy of your progress in your own iCloud account, so it can come back after a reinstall or on a new device. We do not run a server that receives your saved game, and we cannot read your iCloud copy.
  • To keep daily rewards and the offline allowance fair, the game asks a Google server for the current time, or this website if Google does not answer. The request carries no identifier.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Where Google's consent message is required, including the European Economic Area and the UK, it asks for your choices first. On iPhone and iPad, Apple's tracking prompt asks before ads can use your advertising identifier.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Remote Config and Firebase A/B Testing help us balance the game, fix crashes and test changes. This information is linked to identifiers for your device or installation, so it is pseudonymous, not anonymous, and the App Store privacy label lists it as "linked to you". You can switch off "Share gameplay and crash data" in Settings at any time. Where Google's consent message is required, analytics and crash reports stay off until you consent.
  • Apple or Google handles every payment. We never see your card or bank details.
  • Magnet Mob is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Linked to you. The game asks for no name, email or account. Even so, the App Store privacy label and the app's own privacy file list the data sent to Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics and Google AdMob as "linked to you", because each is tied to an identifier for your device or installation: how you use the game, advertising data, purchase history, gameplay, crash and performance data, other diagnostics, a rough location from your IP address, and the device's identifiers. Where you allow tracking in Apple's prompt, the advertising identifier is used for tracking.

Events that Magnet Mob writes itself contain game information, such as a level number or a power used. They never contain your name or email address. The game checks whether your device is online through the operating system; that check sends nothing to us. Haptics use your device's vibration and stay on the device.

Some advertising uses may count as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information, or as targeted advertising, under the laws of some US states. Where that law applies, Google's consent message lets you opt out for the ads shown in the game.

Your choices

  • Share gameplay and crash data: a switch in Settings. It is on when you install the game, except that where Google's consent message is required, including the European Economic Area and the UK, analytics and crash reports stay off until you consent. An explicit saved opt-out remains off when a device restore is incomplete. Turning the switch off stops new events and crash reports and deletes reports still waiting on your device. Information already sent cannot be recalled.
  • Privacy choices: where Google requires it, Settings has a Privacy choices row that opens Google's consent form, where you can review or change your ad and data consent at any time.
  • Apple tracking permission (iPhone and iPad): the game asks before your activity can be tracked across other companies' apps and websites. If you allow it, Google Mobile Ads and Google Analytics for Firebase may use your device's advertising identifier. Declining does not stop you playing and does not remove ads; the ads are less personalised. Change it any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID: reset or delete it in your device's Google settings under Ads.
  • Rewarded videos: always optional. The reward is shown before you choose to watch.
  • Purchases and ads: No Intrusive Ads, the Starter Pack or the Second Builder turns off full-screen ads, banners and the ad at game start. Rewarded videos stay available if you want them. Gold, skips and looks do not turn ads off.
  • Reminders: optional local notifications. The game asks for notification permission only after your first boss win, sends at most one reminder a day, and you can switch each kind of reminder off, or all of them in your device's notification settings.
  • Your progress: Settings > Reset progress starts the game again and keeps your settings and purchases. Deleting the app removes its data from the device. There is no Magnet Mob account. On iPhone and iPad the iCloud copy described above stays in your iCloud account after the app is deleted, following your iCloud settings; on any device, its own backup or transfer can include the game's data, depending on your operating system and settings.

Retention and security

Progress, settings and purchase records stay on your device until you reset them or delete the app, apart from the iCloud copy on iPhone and iPad and any copy in your device's own backups, which follow your iCloud and backup settings. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to answer the request, protect the game or meet legal obligations.

Google Analytics keeps event data linked to an installation for 14 months; aggregated reports can be kept longer. Firebase states that Crashlytics keeps crash traces and their installation identifiers for 90 days. Other Firebase and Google services have their own retention rules, explained in Privacy and security in Firebase.

We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children

Magnet Mob is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us so we can look into it and delete it where appropriate. The store age rating describes the game's content and may differ from this intended audience.

International processing

Google, Apple, GitHub and email providers may process information in countries other than your own, under their own transfer arrangements.

Your rights and contact

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of personal information, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, or to opt out of certain advertising uses. Withdraw analytics and crash-reporting consent with "Share gameplay and crash data" in Settings, and change ad and data consent in Settings > Privacy choices. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before.

Email pranavgarg@gmail.com with a privacy request. We may need some information to verify it. Because the game has no account, we may not be able to match a pseudonymous analytics or advertising record to you without more details.

We will update this page before the game's practices change, for example if we add accounts, leaderboards with other players, push notifications or a new service provider, and we will give any further notice the law requires.
