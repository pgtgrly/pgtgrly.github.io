Progress and saved games

Your progress is saved on your device. There is no Magnet Mob account or cloud save. Deleting the app removes its data from the device, although your device's own backup or transfer may include it. Settings > Reset progress starts the game again and keeps your settings and purchases.

Gold, stars, upgrades, unlocks and other in-game items are entertainment features with no cash value. They cannot be sold, transferred or exchanged for money. We may rebalance, correct or reset them when needed to fix defects, cheating, corrupted saves or balance problems.

Fair play

Every reward is shown before you choose an ad or a chest. If you keep losing a level, the game eases that level slightly. Difficulty never increases because you upgraded or paid. The game has no energy limits on play.

Ads and network access

The game may show optional rewarded videos, full-screen interstitials at eligible breaks between levels, banners on menu and result screens or in a reserved bottom strip during a run, and, for some returning players, an app-open ad on the loading screen. Banners never cover the play area or controls, and full-screen ads do not interrupt a run. Watching a rewarded video is always optional and the reward is shown first; a reward is given only after the ad provider confirms the video finished. Ad availability depends on your region, your privacy choices and the ad provider.

Without a connection you can play a limited number of campaign levels each day; the game shows how many are left, and a level you have started always finishes. If ads cannot load because they are blocked, the same daily limit applies. The limit does not apply once you have made any purchase. Rift Run, ads, purchases, restoring purchases, analytics and crash reporting need an internet connection. Your mobile carrier or internet provider may charge for data use.

Purchases

The game offers two optional one-time purchases: Remove Ads, and a Starter Pack that includes No Ads, a gold bundle and the Gilded Banner skin. Neither is a subscription. The App Store or Google Play shows the localised price before you confirm. Any purchase turns off full-screen ads, banners and the ad at game start; rewarded videos stay available if you want them. The banner skin changes only how your army looks.

Apple or Google handles payment, purchase approval and refunds under its own policies. A refunded or revoked purchase no longer unlocks its content. Use Restore purchases in Settings with the same Apple Account or Google account to restore a purchase after reinstalling or on a new device.

Health and accessibility

The game uses motion, flashes, camera shake, sound and vibration. Settings has controls for sound effects, music, haptics, reduced motion and graphics quality. Take a break or stop playing if you feel discomfort.
