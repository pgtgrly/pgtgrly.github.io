Magnet Mob is a portrait 3D crowd runner. Steer a blue medieval army down the road, pick the better number gate, fight red skeleton armies, pull down archer towers and beat giant bosses. Your Lodestone magnet has two powers, Attract and Repel, and every threat in the game has a way to use them.

Pick the gate. Pull the tower.

Every level is a short run down the road: 30 story levels across three ages, then endless Conquest levels. Grow the mob, read what is coming, and choose the magnet power that solves it.

  • Grow the mob

    Plus and times gates add soldiers. Minus and divide gates take them away. Read both panels before you commit. Some gates climb when you shoot them, and lock gates hold a transformation you can break open.

  • Attract

    Pull enemies into a crushing vortex ahead of your army. Attract also grabs a tower's iron base, drags explosive barrels into enemy packs and yanks shields out of the way.

  • Repel

    Blast a shockwave out from the mob. Repel throws enemies back, sends arrow volleys flying back at their tower, smashes rolling boulders and shoves barrels away before they blow.

  • Pull down archer towers

    Skeleton crossbowmen fire from towers that block a lane. March into the base and your soldiers haul the tower down, free the captives inside and collect its gold.

  • Beat giant bosses

    Every boss shows its attacks before they land and has a weak window: a weapon stuck in the ground, a shield that cracks, a core that opens. Your magnet opens or stretches each one.

  • Transform the army

    Break a lock gate to become Iron Guard, Overdrive or Titan Fusion for a few seconds: heavier armour, much faster fire, or a giant that stomps through enemy packs.

  • Rift Run

    An endless road through the eras, scored in metres. It opens once you beat the Iron Warlord, needs an internet connection, and every run builds a new road.

  • Fair by design

    Authored encounters give threats a visible warning and wind-up before they can hurt you; exact timing varies by encounter. There are no energy timers on play. If you keep losing a level, it eases a little; it never gets harder because you upgraded or paid.

What is Magnet Mob: Crowd Army Runner?
Magnet Mob is a 3D crowd runner game for phones, played in portrait. You steer a blue army through number gates that grow or shrink it, fight red enemy armies, pull down archer towers and battle giant bosses across three ages: the medieval Iron Realm, the futuristic Chrome Rift and the mythic Bronze Age. The campaign has 30 story levels, and two magnet powers, Attract and Repel, are the core skill.
Is Magnet Mob free?
Yes. Magnet Mob is free to download and free to play. It has ads and optional purchases: No Intrusive Ads, a Starter Pack, banner skins, skip packs, a Second Builder and gold. The skins change how the army looks, not how it fights. There are no energy timers. Store listings will show localized prices when they are available.
Does Magnet Mob have ads?

Yes. Every reward video is your choice, and its button says what you get, such as rallying a beaten army, more gold, or one rare card in Rift Run.

Full-screen interstitials appear only at eligible breaks between levels; they do not appear during a run or straight after a revive. An app-open ad may appear on the loading screen for some returning players, subject to pacing and availability. A banner can appear on menus, results, or in a reserved bottom strip during a run; it never covers the play area or controls.

Can I remove ads?
Yes. No Intrusive Ads, the Starter Pack or the Second Builder turns off full-screen ads, banners and the ad at game start, for good. Gold, skips and looks do not. Rewarded videos stay available if you still want their rewards. If you reinstall or change phones, use Restore purchases in Settings.
Does Magnet Mob work offline?
Partly. Without a connection you can play a few campaign levels a day, and the game shows how many are left. A level you have started continues. Rift Run, rewarded videos, purchases and restoring purchases need the internet, and reward buttons show "Connect for bonus" while you are offline. If you have bought anything, offline campaign play has no daily limit. Progress is stored on your device when storage is available; a storage failure or force-close before a checkpoint can prevent the latest progress from surviving a reload.
Which devices does Magnet Mob support?
Magnet Mob is coming to iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 16.4 or later, and to Android phones with Android 7.0 or later. It is played in portrait with one finger.
How do magnet powers work?

Your army carries the Lodestone, a magnet with two powers. Tap Attract (the left button) to pull enemies into a vortex ahead of the mob, where they are crushed together. Tap Repel (the right button) to fire a shockwave that throws enemies back and knocks incoming shots away.

Both powers share one magnet meter. It fills as you defeat enemies, pass energy gates and pick up scrap, and a short cooldown follows each use. Repel costs a little more than Attract. Level 1 ends with your first Attract. Attract is taught on level 2, and Repel opens on level 9.

Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up or login. Your progress and settings stay on your device.
